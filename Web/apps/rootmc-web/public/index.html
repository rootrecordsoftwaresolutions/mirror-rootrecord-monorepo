About RootMC

A community-first survival realm

RootMC is a long-form Minecraft survival server built around cooperation, trade, and territory — not pay-to-win shortcuts. You earn gold through play, list items in player shops, found Towny towns, and grow skills with mcMMO while the world evolves with the people who live in it.

What makes it different

  • Player economy — Gold (G) comes from minting, selling, and trading. The stock market and shop browser show real listings synced from the live server.
  • Towny & nations — Claim plots, elect mayors, form nations, and get dedicated Discord channels as your settlement grows.
  • McMMO progression — Classic skill grinding with public stats once you link your account.
  • Live map — Explore builds at map.rootmc.net while you plan your next base.
  • Discord bridge — In-game chat mirrors to Discord; community votes and weekly awards keep both sides connected.

Fair play & linking

We expect respect, no cheating (x-ray, dupes, unfair clients), and honest trade. Linking your Minecraft account to RootRecord unlocks the linked-player Discord role, public stats pages, and the RootMC Android companion app. Full rules and commands live in the player wiki.

Weekly recognition

Every Sunday at 08:00 HST we post Top Participator (Discord messages, votes, and reactions) and Top Active Player (linked in-game playtime). Roles reset each week — when awards are posted, the leaders appear in Discord and on this page.
