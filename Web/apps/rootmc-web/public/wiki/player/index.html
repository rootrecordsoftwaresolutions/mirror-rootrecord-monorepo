Overview

RootMC is a public survival multiplayer server. Economy, towns, and skills run on Root-Essentials, Towny, and mcMMO. The RootMC plugin and rootmc-shops link accounts, sync stats, power chest shops, and feed Root Shops on the web and in the RootMC Android app. Root-Loans adds optional personal borrowing after a community vote.

When you play here you get:

  • Gold-backed economy — balance in G; mining and selling gold is the main income loop.
  • Personal loans — borrow G for startup costs (e.g. founding a town); repay via income sweep or manually.
  • Towny — claim land, grow towns and nations.
  • mcMMO — slower, long-term skill progression mirrored to the web after you link.
  • Player shops — rootmc-shops chest shops (QuickShop read during cutover).
  • Account linking — tie Minecraft to Discord (and optionally email) for stats, web tools, and RootMC app sync.
  • In-game capture — /rootmc waypoint and /rootmc note sync to the app.
  • Root Shops — live prices, listings, net worth, stock market, and offline buy → in-game vault.
World RootMC · connect at play.rootmc.net (Java Edition). Join RootMC Discord for announcements and support.

Join the server

  1. Launch Minecraft Java Edition on a compatible version (currently 26.1).
  2. Join play.rootmc.net (or ask in RootMC Discord if the address changes).
  3. Open Multiplayer → Add Server → enter the announced address and any name you like (e.g. RootMC).
  4. Join and play. No mod pack is required for linking or stats — vanilla client is fine.

If you cannot connect, check that your game version matches the server and that your firewall allows outbound connections to port 25565. Ask in RootMC Discord if the server is down for maintenance.

First steps checklist

  1. Spawn in, set a home, and play normally — nothing special is required on day one.
  2. Run /rootmc link when you want your character linked to Discord and the website.
  3. Complete verification at rootmc.net/verify — enter your code and link with Discord.
  4. Run /rootmc status to confirm linking and copy your public stats URL.
  5. Optional: install the RootMC Android app and sign in with the same linked account.
  6. Mine and /sell ore — gold ingots are worth about 1 G each.
  7. Optional: need 400 G to found a town? See personal loans.
  8. Optional: browse Root Shops for live prices before you list items.

Economy (G)

RootMC uses Root-Essentials + Vault. Your balance shows as G in chat, shops, and Towny costs.

Earning G

  • /sell or sell signs — item values in root-essentials worth config; 1 gold ingot ≈ 1 G.
  • Player shops — sell to other players via QuickShop.
  • Town plots and jobs in the wild — trading, services, etc.

Spending G

  • Towny — founding a town, claiming plots, nations, outposts (see below).
  • QuickShop — buying from player chest shops.
  • Death tax — a small percent of balance on death (capped).
  • Loan repayment — optional manual pay-down with /loan repay (see personal loans).

New players start at 0 G — plan to mine gold before founding a town (400 G to create one), or borrow up to your loan limit with /loan take.

Personal loans (Root-Loans)

After a community vote in favor, RootMC runs Root-Loans — optional personal borrowing in G. One active loan per player. Useful for startup costs (e.g. 400 G to found a town) when you are short on gold.

How borrowing works

  • /loan take <amount> — borrow up to your current limit. Cash lands in your Vault balance immediately.
  • 10% interest — you owe principal + 10% (borrow 50 G → owe 55 G).
  • One loan at a time — pay off before taking another.
  • 3 borrows per rolling 24 hours — after the third, wait until the oldest borrow ages out.

Credit limit

RuleValue
Starting borrow limit50 G
After each full payoffLimit × 1.1 (rounded to server math)
Hard cap500 G
Interest rate10% on each borrow

Run /loan info anytime for amount owed, your limit, and how many borrows you have used in the last 24 hours.

Repayment

While you owe money, 100% of incoming gold income is applied to the loan first — whatever is left goes to your wallet. This includes:

  • /sell and /mint
  • Shop sales and player payments received
  • Playtime / vote rewards
  • Other Vault deposits routed through Root-Essentials

Gold ore while in debt: breaking gold ore (overworld, deepslate, or nether) does not drop gold items — the gold value is applied straight to your loan. Non-gold drops from the block still drop normally.

Manual repay: /loan repay pays the full remaining balance from your wallet. /loan repay <amount> pays a partial amount.

Loans are closed-economy credit — the server does not print free money beyond what you borrow. Pay off on time to grow your limit toward the 500 G cap.

Towns & land (Towny)

Towny lets residents claim chunks for a town and set permissions. RootMC has no daily town or plot taxes — you pay one-time costs to found a town and claim land, not recurring upkeep.

Basics

  • /town new <name> — found a town (400 G).
  • /town claim — claim the chunk you stand in (cost scales per claim).
  • /town deposit <amount> — add G to the town bank (nations, upgrades).
  • /plot claim — personal plot inside a town when enabled by mayor.
  • /towny map — see nearby town borders.

What to expect

Rule of thumbValue
New town cost400 G
New nation cost2000 G
Plots per resident (max)24
Daily plot/town taxesNone
Closed economyNo free money from the server — earn via play

Full numbers live in server config; staff may tune one-time fees over time.

Link your account

Linking proves you own the Minecraft account. Run /rootmc link in-game for a short code, then finish on the website. The server picks up the result within a few minutes.

Step by step

  1. In-game, run /rootmc link.
  2. Chat shows a verification code and URL (expires in about 15 minutes).
  3. On your phone or PC, open rootmc.net/verify.
  4. Enter the code and choose Link with Discord (recommended).
  5. Back in-game, run /rootmc status — you should see linked status and your public stats link.
One linked Minecraft UUID per Discord account. If you already linked, /rootmc link tells you instead of issuing a new code.

Why link?

  • Public stats page with McMMO snapshot.
  • Linked-player role and in-game nickname on RootMC Discord.
  • RootMC app sync (when signed in with the same link).
  • In-game waypoints and notes appear in the app after sync.
  • Your shop signs can attribute listings to your username on Root Shops.

Community votes

RootMC runs player governance on Discord — major changes (like enabling Root-Loans) go through a public vote. Only linked Minecraft accounts count.

How it works

  1. Staff opens a vote with /proposal create — a thread appears in the **operations forum** with **For / Against / Abstain** buttons.
  2. A ping goes to #general so everyone can find it.
  3. Link at rootmc.net/verify, then vote in the thread.
  4. After the deadline (or staff /proposal close), results post to #general.

/proposal list and /proposal status id:<id> show open votes and tallies. Pass requires at least 5 linked voters and more **For** than **Against**.

Season arcs

RootMC can run **community-approved season arcs** — themed periods with extra rotating in-game tips. Staff opens a vote with /proposal create and sets kind: Season arc, plus an optional theme and custom lines (pipe-separated, e.g. &6Trade week|&7Visit player shops).

What happens when a season vote passes

  1. The new season is stored on RootMC cloud and announced in Discord #general.
  2. Root-Announcer pulls season lines from GET /api/rootmc/season every few minutes and merges them with the normal tip rotation.
  3. Only one season is active at a time — a new passed vote replaces the previous arc.

Check the active season on the web via the server config API or ask staff with /proposal status on the vote that created it.

Player contracts (escrow)

Hire another player with Gold held in escrow until you approve the work — no trust trades required.

  1. /contract offer <player> <amount> <terms...> — pulls G from your balance into escrow; worker must be online.
  2. Worker runs /contract accept <id>.
  3. When satisfied, client runs /contract complete <id> — releases Gold to the worker.
  4. Client can /contract cancel <id> before completion for a full refund.

/contract list shows your open offers and active jobs. Limits: 1–10,000 G per contract (configurable), max 5 open per player.

Your public stats page

After linking, /rootmc status prints a URL like:

https://rootmc.net/player/?uuid=<your-minecraft-uuid>

Or search by username at rootmc.net/player.

Anyone with the link can view:

  • Minecraft username and UUID
  • Whether the account is linked
  • McMMO skill levels (updated when the server syncs)
  • Shared worlds on your profile (if you use the app)

Stats refresh on a schedule (about every 5 minutes) and when operators run /rootmc sync. They are not real-time tick-by-tick.

Weekly activity awards

Every Sunday 08:00 HST, RootMC posts weekly leaders in Discord and on the home page:

  • Top Participator (top 5) — Discord message blocks, votes (5 pts each), reactions; judged by Root-AI.
  • Top Active Player (top 5) — weekly in-game playtime for linked accounts; includes one week of Pro on the app.

McMMO on the web

RootMC runs mcMMO for skills (mining, woodcutting, combat, etc.). Progression is tuned for a longer grind than default — diminished returns after heavy play in one session, lower global XP, and mining pays less than vanilla mcMMO.

RootMC reads mcMMO’s MySQL tables (no extra install). Skill levels on your public stats page mirror in-game data after the next sync (~5 minutes) or when staff run /rootmc sync. Link first or stats stay empty on the web.

Root Shops (economy browser)

Root Shops is the public web view of player shops on RootMC. RootMC collects listings from rootmc-shops first, then QuickShop (during cutover), ChestShop if present, then sign shops in loaded chunks.

On the shops page you can see:

  • Average prices — rolling average per item from active listings
  • Shop listings — item, price, world, coordinates, owner when known
  • Net worth leaderboard — Vault balance plus valued inventory (periodic snapshot)

In-game, run /rootmc shops to print the share URL. Filter by player with ?player=<uuid> on the web.

Opening a player shop (rootmc-shops)

Create a sell shop

  1. Place a chest or barrel (leave room on the side you face for an auto-placed shop sign).
  2. Hold the item you sell in your hand — stack size sets how many sell per purchase.
  3. Left-click the container, or shift+right-click it.
  4. Type the total price for one purchase in chat (cancel to abort). Nothing is taken from your hand.
  5. Put stock in the container after the shop is created.

Buy from a shop

  • Right-click a listed shop chest — buys one stack at the listed price.
  • /buy <ITEM> [amount] — buys from the cheapest in-stock listing server-wide (up to 2304).

Manage your shop

  • /rootshops editprice · /rootshops editqty — look at your shop sign, then type the new value in chat.
  • /rootshops remove — look at your shop chest and remove the listing.
  • /rootshops cancel — abort a pending price entry.
  • /rootshops avg <ITEM> — show rolling average for an item.

QuickShop (legacy / cutover)

During migration, existing QuickShop-Hikari shops may still appear on Root Shops until recreated with rootmc-shops. New spawn shops should use rootmc-shops only.

Sign shops (fallback scan)

If you use raw signs instead, Root Shops still discovers them when the format matches:

LineContent
Line 1 (recommended)Your Minecraft name Steve or @Steve — attributes the listing to you on the web
Any lineItem in brackets, e.g. [DIAMOND] or [Oak Log] — normalized to a material name
Any linePrice number, e.g. 12.5 or 100
OptionalWord buy on a line marks a buy shop; otherwise treated as sell

Example sign:

@Alex
[DIAMOND]
Sell 50 each
50

Price cap (fair market)

New rootmc-shops listings and sign placements are blocked when price exceeds 10% above the current server rolling average for that item (when an average exists). You’ll see a chat message with the max allowed price.

rootmc-shops listings sync to Root Shops on the economy heartbeat (~5 min) when at least one player is online — no chunk scan needed. Legacy QuickShop or raw sign shops only appear if their chunk was loaded during a world scan.

The RootMC Android app (Google Play) is the mobile companion — coordinates, worlds, stats, shops, stock market, and vault. Not required to play, but pairs with a linked account:

FAQ

Do I need to link an account to play?

No. Linking is optional but unlocks Discord perks, web stats, RootMC app sync, and vault/stock market.

How often does the website update?

Typically every 5 minutes from scheduled sync, plus when staff run /rootmc sync.

Why isn’t my shop on the website?

First confirm you used rootmc-shops: hold the item, left-click or shift+right-click the chest or barrel, type the total price in chat, then stock the container. /rootshops create only prints a reminder — it does not set a price.

Listings push to Root Shops on the economy sync (~5 min) when at least one player is online (joining triggers a sync). Staff can run /rootmc sync to push sooner. Legacy QuickShop or sign-only shops need their chunk loaded during a scan — new shops should use rootmc-shops.

Why are my mcMMO stats empty on the web?

Link with /rootmc link first, then wait for sync. mcMMO must be writing to MySQL (default on RootMC).

How hard is mcMMO here?

Slower than default — plan for long-term progression. Grinding one skill all day hits diminished returns.

How do personal loans work?

Run /loan take <amount> up to your limit (starts at 50 G, grows after each full payoff, caps at 500 G). You owe principal + 10% interest. While in debt, incoming gold income pays the loan first; mining gold ore also pays down debt. See Personal loans.

Is my inventory public?

Only economy totals (balances, item totals for leaderboard math) go to the cloud — not a full chest-by-chest map. Listings only include sign data you place in the world.

Who do I contact for help?

RootMC Discord — dedicated Minecraft server community (guild ID 1516108585740800042).
