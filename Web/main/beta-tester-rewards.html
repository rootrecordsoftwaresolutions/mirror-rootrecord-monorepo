RootRecord Account Billing & Pro plans

How it works (summary)

Rewards are earned while you are signed in and using the app in good faith. The system measures session activity (including time in different parts of the app) and may apply per-screen limits so the program stays fair. Daily earning limits apply per app (each participating app has its own cap for the calendar day), so using more than one app can add up to more than a single app’s limit. Your Root Units balance on this page is your running total across the program. From time to time a one-time daily check-in bonus may be available in each app; the app will show if you have already claimed it for that app today.

Exact rates, caps, and eligibility can change during the beta. We will keep this page updated when something material changes.

Redemption details

Redeeming 100,000 Root Units debits your balance and extends your Pro membership by 30 days. If you already have an active Pro window (paid subscription, lifetime plan, or a previous redemption that hasn't expired), the 30 days are added on top of your current expiration — you don't lose any time. Pro perks unlock immediately on this account across web and Android apps.

Your account

Root Unit balances are associated with the same email you use to sign in on Account. If you have billing or subscription questions, that is separate from this program—see Billing when you need to manage a paid plan.
