HOW TO INSTALL (CLOSED TESTING)
————————————————————————————————————————

1. Join this group: https://groups.google.com/g/rootrecordtesting
2. On your Android device, open the “Join closed testing” link for the app (above).
3. Sign in with the same Google account you use on the Play Store.
4. Accept the test, then install or update from the Play Store.
5. Sign in inside the app with your rootrecord.info account.
