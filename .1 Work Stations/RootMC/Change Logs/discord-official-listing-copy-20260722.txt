### Long description
**The RootMC Network** is the official home of RootMC — two linked survival worlds (**Towny** and **Claims**) on a modern Paper stack, built for long-form play, honest trade, and territory you can grow into.

**Play**
Join at `play.rootmc.net`. Towny hosts towns and nations; Claims hosts personal land. Progression — playtime, ranks, votes, and related stats — travels with you between the official pair. Gold wallets and inventories stay per-host.

**Economy**
Gold (G) is a closed-loop currency: mint from ore, trade in shops, borrow and bond, and redeem toward physical gold at a published peg. The Server Reserve collects fees and redistributes through treasury grants — no money faucet, no pay-to-win. Rules live in the Constitution; tools live on the site (market, economy lists, reserve views).

**Community & tools**
Discord bridges chat and community life. Link Minecraft for public stats, roles, and the companion app. A live map, leaderboards, activity/time views, and weekly recognition keep the network visible outside the client.

**For operators**
The same first-party plugin suite that runs on official hosts is published on rootmc.net — catalog jars update when those servers deploy.
