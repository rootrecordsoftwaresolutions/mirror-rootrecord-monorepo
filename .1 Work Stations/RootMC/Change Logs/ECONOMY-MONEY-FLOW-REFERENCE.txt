SECTION 4 — TREASURY LEDGER: INFLOWS vs OUTFLOWS
================================================================================

INFLOWS (credits reserve balance + ledger):
  TAX              — transaction tax, inactivity tax, closed-loop tax portion
  DEATH            — death fee reserve share (40% default)
  TOWNY_SINK       — net Towny fees, rank purchases, service fees after tax
  LOAN_PRINCIPAL   — loan repayment principal portion
  LOAN_INTEREST    — loan repayment interest portion
  BOND_ISSUE       — player parks G in bonded notes
  BOND_COUPON_FORFEIT — unclaimed bond earnings returned
  DONATION         — /pay reserve (player burns Notes → reserve credit)
  OPENING          — baseline / migration entries

OUTFLOWS (debits reserve):
  GRANT            — staff /grant, vote, playtime, Discord, app, map return, etc.
  VOTE             — (also tagged GRANT in plugin paths; ledger may show VOTE)
  DIVIDEND         — monthly activity dividend payouts
  LOAN_DISBURSE    — personal or town loan funding
  BOND_REDEEM      — bond note cashed out
  BOND_COUPON      — bond earnings paid to wallet

NEUTRAL / SUPPLY:
  NOTE_BURN        — tax destroyed while over-issued; /mint gold redemption
  OTHER            — manual corrections

SECTION 5 — MONEY MOVEMENT PATHS (CODE & BEHAVIOR)
================================================================================

5.1 PLAYER-TO-PLAYER /pay
    PayCommand → RootEssentialsPlugin.transfer()
    1. gross = amount player sends
    2. tax = gross * effectiveTransactionTaxRate(), rounded
    3. Debit payer gross from root_economy_balances
    4. Credit towny-server tax (TAX) OR burn (NOTE_BURN) if over-issued
    5. Credit recipient (gross - tax) via depositIncome()
    6. If active loan: 100% of net income sweeps to loan first

5.2 SHOP BUY (P2P)
    ShopBuyService → ShopEconomy.withholdTaxedPayment()
    Same tax pattern as /pay: buyer debited gross, seller gets net, tax to reserve/burn

5.3 SHOP STOCK FEE
    Player → 5.000 G via closed-loop (settleClosedLoopPayment) → reserve

5.4 TOWNY CLOSED-LOOP FEES
    TownyTreasuryBridge tags channel (towny:claim, towny:new-town, etc.)
    Vault withdraw from player/town → TreasuryManager.settleClosedLoopPayment()
    1. tax → TAX or NOTE_BURN
    2. net → TOWNY_SINK (full fee lands in reserve, not destroyed except tax burn mode)

5.5 DEATH FEE
    DeathTreasuryListener → TreasuryManager.settleDeathFee()
    → EconomyStore.applyDeathFee() atomic victim/killer/treasury update

5.6 TREASURY GRANT (automated & staff)
    TreasuryManager.grantToPlayer()
    → EconomyStore.grantFromTreasury() debits towny-server, credits player
    → Ledger GRANT (or VOTE for vote-specific paths)
    → depositIncome() may sweep to loan

5.7 LOAN TAKE
    LoanService.takeLoan() → treasury.disburseLoan()
    Reserve ↓ principal | Player ↑ principal | Ledger LOAN_DISBURSE
    Owed recorded = principal * 1.10

5.8 LOAN REPAY
    treasury.receiveLoanRepayment(principal, interest)
    Player ↓ | Reserve ↑ | Ledger LOAN_PRINCIPAL + LOAN_INTEREST

5.9 BOND CREATE
    BondService.createBond(): player wallet ↓ | reserve ↑ BOND_ISSUE | paper note

5.10 BOND EARNINGS
    Daily MC day: 25% of reserve inflows × (player notes / total notes) → wallet

5.11 /MINT (physical → wallet)
    MintCommand → mintIncomeAfterTax(): tax withheld, net to wallet, mint:gross audit

5.12 /MINT GOLD (wallet → physical)
    Burns wallet G, spawns gold items, NOTE_BURN ledger mint:redeem=

5.13 CONTRACT
    Offer: client wallet ↓ full amount (escrow in logic, not separate account)
    Complete: worker wallet ↑ full amount
    Cancel: client wallet ↑ refund

5.14 PENDING API TRANSFERS
    API inserts rootmc_gold_transfers (pending)
    EconomyCollector polls → grantFromTreasury or vault.transfer → mark complete

5.15 INACTIVITY TAX
    Each MC day: InactivityTaxService → withdraw from target → creditTreasury TAX

5.16 UNCLAIM REFUND
    TownyPlotRefundBridge → grantToPlayer(town bank, from treasury, towny:unclaim-refund)

5.17 RANK PURCHASE
    RankPurchaseService.sinkToTreasury() → settleClosedLoopPayment()

5.18 MYSQL → D1 SYNC
    Cron rootmc-mysql-economy-pull.ts: balances, ledger, playtime, net worth → D1

SECTION 7 — FULL WORKED EXAMPLE (DECIMAL-LEVEL)
================================================================================

Scenario: Reserve ledger net = 500.000 G → tax tier = 1% (0.010).
Players: Alexrs94 (wallet 250.000 G), Melee__ (wallet 80.000 G).
Town: Valhalla (bank town-Valhalla, 1,200.000 G).
Assume Alexrs94 is mayor, no active loan, Explorer rank.

--- EVENT A: Alexrs94 /pay Melee__ 100.000 G ---
  gross           = 100.000 G
  tax rate        = 0.010 (1%)
  tax             = round(100.000 * 0.010) = 1.000 G
  net to Melee__  = 99.000 G

  Alexrs94 wallet:  250.000 - 100.000 = 150.000 G
  Melee__ wallet:    80.000 +  99.000 = 179.000 G
  towny-server:      +1.000 G (TAX ledger, channel pay)

--- EVENT B: Valhalla claims 1 town plot (/t claim) ---
  Towny charges: 12.000 G (increase=1.0 → flat)
  Closed-loop from town bank (or mayor if broke):

  tax  = round(12.000 * 0.010) = 0.120 G
  sink = 12.000 - 0.120       = 11.880 G

  town-Valhalla: 1,200.000 - 12.000 = 1,188.000 G
  towny-server:  +0.120 TAX + 11.880 TOWNY_SINK = +12.000 G total

--- EVENT C: Valhalla unclaims that plot (/t unclaim) ---
  Refund marginal cost = 12.000 G (not bonus-block purchase)
  towny-server → town-Valhalla grant 12.000 G (towny:unclaim-refund)

  town-Valhalla: 1,188.000 + 12.000 = 1,200.000 G
  towny-server:  -12.000 G (GRANT outflow — net Towny claim is tax only)

--- EVENT D: Alexrs94 buys Wanderer rank (/rank buy) — price 500.000 G ---
  gross = 500.000 G
  tax   = round(500.000 * 0.010) = 5.000 G
  sink  = 495.000 G

  Alexrs94: 150.000 - 500.000 → FAIL (insufficient; needs 500.000+)
  If wallet were 600.000 G:
    Alexrs94: 600.000 - 500.000 = 100.000 G
    towny-server: +5.000 TAX + 495.000 TOWNY_SINK

--- EVENT E: Melee__ dies (mob), wallet 179.000 G ---
  percentFee = round(179.000 * 0.15) = 26.850 G
  fee = max(1.000, 26.850) = 26.850 G
  No PvP killer → all to treasury

  Melee__ wallet: 179.000 - 26.850 = 152.150 G
  towny-server:   +26.850 G (DEATH ledger)

--- EVENT F: Melee__ takes personal loan 100.000 G ---
  interest 10% → owed = 110.000 G
  towny-server: -100.000 LOAN_DISBURSE
  Melee__ wallet: 152.150 + 100.000 = 252.150 G (then owes 110.000)

--- EVENT G: Melee__ receives vote reward 15.000 G (app, fixed site) ---
  Pending transfer → grant from treasury
  Income sweep 100% to loan first:
    Applied to loan: min(15.000, 110.000 owed) = 15.000 G
    Wallet increase: 0.000 G
    Loan owed: 110.000 - 15.000 = 95.000 G
  towny-server: -15.000 GRANT

--- EVENT H: Alexrs94 /mint hand — 9 gold ingots ---
  gross mint = 9 * 1.000 = 9.000 G
  tax = round(9.000 * 0.010) = 0.090 G
  net = 8.910 G

  Physical gold removed from inventory
