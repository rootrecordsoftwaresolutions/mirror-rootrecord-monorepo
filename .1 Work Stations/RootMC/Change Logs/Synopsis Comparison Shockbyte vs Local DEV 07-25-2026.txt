================================================================================
1. SYNOPSIS
================================================================================

Shockbyte hosts are live mainnet (Claims + Towny). This workstation runs a
local DEV / Local Testing Paper stack on Desktop SSD — a testnet peer on the
RootMC Node network, not a replacement for production.

After the Desktop SSD move + warm MySQL, local Done dropped from ~197 s to
~60 s (~3.3× faster). Shockbyte still boots faster (dedicated 2 vCPU + warm
prod MySQL), but local is now in the same order of magnitude as Towny Stage A
rather than ~7× slower.

================================================================================
4. RESOURCE READ (steady / post-ready; boot spikes ignored)
================================================================================

  Same 2 vCPU on both Shockbyte hosts: neither is CPU-bound when quiet after
  restart. Towny carried real load pre-restart (~40–60% CPU); Claims was idle
  (~3–5%).

  Absolute RAM working sets sit in a similar ballpark (~0.5–1.4 GB) across
  Claims, Towny, and local Paper after Done. Towny’s 6 GB allotment is mostly
  headroom; Claims packs denser on 2 GB; local uses a 3 GB heap cap on a 32 GB
  desktop.
