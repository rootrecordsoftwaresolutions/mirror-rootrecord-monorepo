INTERNAL TESTING (PLAY STORE) & HOW TO JOIN
================================================================================

These links only work if you are an internal tester with RootRecord Software
Systems.

Join this Google group for permissions:
https://groups.google.com/u/1/g/rootrecordtesting

Business Manager (Play internal testing):
https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.rootrecord.businessmanager

Weather Manager (Play internal testing):
https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.rootrecord.weathermanager

Requirements:
- Download from Google Play after being invited as a tester
- Keep the app installed for 2 weeks
- Provide feedback as you see fit
Rewards to be determined on completion.

Perks:
RootRecord runs a usage-based rewards program for people who help test our
Android apps. Rewards are tracked when you use a participating app while
signed in with your rootrecord.info account.

In test versions, you will see a line like "Beta Tester Rewards" with a number.
That is your current usage reward balance for the program (one running total
across all apps). You can open the rewards page from the app for full context.

Rewards reflect real use of the app while signed in. To keep things fair there
are daily limits per app and per-screen limits. All apps offer a daily
check-in bonus. Rewards are tied to the same email you use for your RootRecord
account. Subscriptions and billing are separate from rewards, but membership
redemption is planned.

If you use RootRecord Solana tools, we provide a custodial wallet for rewards.
We cover network fees when you withdraw earned RRTT to your linked payout
address. You pay on-chain fees if you move SOL, USDC, or other SPL tokens from
that wallet. Details are on the rewards page.

Cash out:
https://solana.rootrecord.info/account
