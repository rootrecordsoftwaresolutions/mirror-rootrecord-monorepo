We’re opening another beta round on **Google Play** (Android). This release is **Business Manager 1.10** with **versionCode 10**

**What this app is**
Mobile workspace for **small business ops**: **dashboard**, **time tracking**, **finance & clients** (money, clients, invoices, debts, funds, tax-related views, and more), **schedule**, plus **More** for **work log**, **categories**, **reports** (including charts / PDF-style export flows), **stock & supplies**, and **settings** (account, business, program, about, feedback, developer messages). Uses a **Root Record account** (or guest where supported) so data can sync with the backend — **not** accounting or legal advice; you’re responsible for your own books and compliance.

**How to join**
1. Join the beta tester group **https://groups.google.com/u/1/g/rootrecordtesting**
2. Open the Play testing link: **https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.rootrecord.businessmanager**
3. Accept the beta, install **Business Manager**, leave auto-update on if you can.

**What to stress-test**
- First launch: sign-in, sign-up, any guest path, and navigation to the main tabs
- **Dashboard**: loads, anything stale or confusing after idle
- **Time tracking**: start/stop, edits, categories, anything that loses time or duplicates entries
- **Finance & Clients**: each sub-tab you use (money, clients, invoices, debts, funds, etc.) — saves, lists, and anything that looks wrong offline/online
- **Schedule**: create/edit items, conflicts or wrong times/time zones
- **More**: work log, reports export, stock flows, developer messages
- General: crashes, jank, wrong strings, dark mode if you use it

**Privacy / permissions (high level)**
Internet (API + sync), **account** (email/password flow), optional **storage / share** if you export or attach files — depends what you tap in-app. Review the in-app privacy / permissions text for the full list.

**How to report issues**
Reply in **#business-manager** with: **device model**, **Android version**, **app version** (Settings / About — or 1.10), **steps**, **screenshots** if UI. For crashes, note roughly **what you tapped** before it died.

Mahalo — your feedback shapes what ships next.
