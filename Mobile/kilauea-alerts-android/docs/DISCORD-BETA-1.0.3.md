We’re opening another beta round on **Google Play** (Android). This release is **Kilauea Alerts 1.0.3** with **versionCode 3**

**What this app is**
Independent companion for **Hawaiʻi Island / Kīlauea**: home dashboard, **earthquakes**, **weather** (saved spots + optional GPS), **live feeds** (official cameras/streams in-app), **USGS volcano notices** and **NWS alerts**, plus **More** for notification settings. **Not** produced or endorsed by USGS or any government agency — data comes from public official sources; in any emergency, follow **civil defense and official instructions**.

**How to join**
1. Open the Play testing link: **[https://groups.google.com/g/rootrecordtesting]**
2. Accept the beta, install **Kilauea Alerts**, leave auto-update on if you can.

**What to stress-test**
- First launch, tutorial skip vs complete
- **Alerts** tab: loads, refresh, anything that looks wrong or hours out of date
- **Notifications** (grant permission): do you get timely pushes when notices change? Any spam or silence?
- **Weather**: saved locations, GPS-based detail, anything odd offline/online
- **Live Feeds**: links open, video pages usable
- **Earthquakes**: list makes sense vs what you see on USGS
- General: crashes, jank, wrong strings, dark mode if you use it

**Privacy / permissions (high level)**
Internet (data), optional **location** (weather/GPS features), **notifications** (alerts). No account required for this beta focus.

**How to report issues**
Reply in **#kilauea** with: **device model**, **Android version**, **app version** (Settings / About if present, or 1.0.3), **steps**, **screenshots** if UI. For crashes, note roughly **what you tapped** before it died.

Mahalo — your feedback shapes what ships next.
